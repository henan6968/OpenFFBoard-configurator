fix: stop the signature reply from raising on every read

The board answers sys.0.signature! with something that is not always a hex
string, and sig_cb called bytes.fromhex() on it. processMatchedReply only
guards callbacks against RuntimeError, so the ValueError escaped, was logged
as "Can not process reply" for every reply that followed, and the callback
was never released.

Parse defensively in a small helper instead: a signature that cannot be
decoded becomes b'', which the existing checks already treat as unsigned.

Verified: launching the real window now produces a completely clean stderr.
